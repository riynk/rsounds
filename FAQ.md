# RSOUNDS — Frequently Asked Questions

RSOUNDS is an Android music player maintained by **riynkkazis**. It is based on the open-source Retro Music codebase. This page covers the RSOUNDS project; older Retro Music beta, purchase and support instructions do not apply to RSOUNDS.

## What is RSOUNDS?

RSOUNDS is a player for audio files available on your Android device. It helps you browse your local music library and play tracks with features such as playlists, themes and lyrics.

## Does RSOUNDS provide music streaming or song downloads?

No. RSOUNDS is a music player, not a music store or streaming catalogue. You are responsible for the audio files you add to your device and for respecting the rights that apply to them.

## Why are some tracks missing from my library?

1. Check that Android has granted RSOUNDS permission to access audio or music on your device.
2. Confirm that the files are visible to Android's media library and can be played by another local music player.
3. Check whether the app's library filters or selected folders are excluding the tracks.
4. After copying or moving music, give Android time to update its media library, then reopen RSOUNDS.

Menu names and permission prompts can vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

## Why will a particular audio file not play?

Playback compatibility can depend on the Android version, device codecs and the file itself. Check that the file plays in another player on the same device. When reporting an issue, include the file format and device details, but do not upload copyrighted audio or private files.

## How do I report a bug?

Use the [RSOUNDS bug report form](https://github.com/riynk/retro_music/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml). Include:
- Device model and Android version.
- RSOUNDS version (shown in **About → Version**).
- Steps to reproduce the problem and what you expected to happen.
- Relevant logs or screenshots, after removing personal information.

Please search existing issues before opening a new report.
